Police Chief: F2P Field Guide

Yotta Games / VoyagerOne 4X strategy game (iOS and Android). Built from community guides as of late October 2026.

Your server is about 4 days old. This tab covers days 4 to 10. Chief’s Office level matters more than the day count, so use it to decide what applies to you.

Do now

  1. Join an active alliance and request help on every timer.
  2. Push Chief’s Office to 7. It unlocks the Intel Center, Security Wall level 2 (10 seconds, cheap) and the cross-server King of the Precinct event.
  3. Use every free recruit, patrol and Arena attempt daily.
  4. Keep both builders, research and all three academies busy.
  5. Don’t spend speedups, Hardened Steel, hero fragments or gear materials yet. See the event plan below.

Right game?

This guide covers the mobile 4X Police Chief (package com.slg.policewar) with Chief’s Office, heroes, alliances and state events. It is not Police Chief Simulator (Steam, Perfect Circle) or the Steam sim Police Chief by Blue Directive.

The game in one minute

  • Base builder plus PvP: upgrade your HQ, train three troop types, collect heroes, gather resources on the state map, and fight in alliance events.
  • Troop triangle: Guard (tank), Biker (also called Rider in some guides), Marksman (damage).
  • Late game uses Electro Cores, which unlock once your server is 70 days old, for building levels past 30.
  • Hero rarities: Legendary (SSR), Epic (SR), Rare (R). Generations: Gen 1, Gen 2, and Gen 3 is announced as coming soon.

Heads-up on PvP

A recent store review says new players get attacked early by bigger players and that retaliating can draw in whole alliances. Join an active alliance fast, keep resources under your protection limit, and don’t leave troops idle outside your base.

Golden rules

  1. Chief’s Office drives everything. Before any upgrade, ask whether it helps the next Chief’s Office level.
  2. Never let a construction, research or training queue sit empty.
  3. Alliance first: help timers, tech, gifts and event rewards beat any solo play.
  4. Spend speedups and Hardened Steel on event days, not randomly.

Why these steps

Chief’s Office 7 = Intel Center

Intel Center gives daily missions and resources. Clear intel cases within the refresh window. Criminal Capture missions also produce Tranquilizers for the alliance Vault Trap.

Second construction queue

Construction becomes the main bottleneck. Guides rank the second queue as one of the earliest premium or Diamond purchases worth making.

Extra squad queues

Researching more squad slots beats most early combat tech: more gatherers and more event participation.

Chief Orders

  • Rapid Construction: use on 8+ hour builds.
  • Supply Coordination: use before big gathering.
  • Advance Preparations: instant resources; combine with Supply Coordination.

Research order

One popular roadmap says Development, then Battle, then Economy. Another early guide says economy first, then combat. Both agree extra squad queues come first. Pick Development early and add Battle once your economy holds.

Send them gathering, not fighting: Doc Zoc (electricity), Ammo Alvin (ammo), Cash Money Cassie (cash), Gas Gus (gas).

Leveling rules

  • Pick one carry per troop type and funnel EXP into it. Spreading evenly wastes it.
  • Don’t spend combat EXP on logistics heroes.
  • Use every free recruitment daily once Hero Hall unlocks; shards matter.

PvE and facilities

Ranger Rix saves stamina on PvE and is good for attacking and seizing facilities. One guide says he isn’t suited to general rallies.

Role rules

  • You can’t stack duplicate hero types in one squad. Mix Guard, Biker and Marksman.
  • Guards soak damage and weaken enemy attack. Marksmen deal damage and control. Bikers lead rallies and buff the squad.
  • Counter triangle: Guard beats Biker, Biker beats Marksman, Marksman beats Guard. An earlier lower-quality source had it reversed, so ignore that. See the Troops & Rallies tab.
  • Slot count differs by source: one shows 2 front and 3 back, another mentions a 2-2-1 builder. Check your own formation screen.

Gathering preset

Make a second preset of Doc Zoc, Ammo Alvin, Cash Money Cassie and Gas Gus. Swap to it before sending gatherers out, then swap back to combat heroes for defense. Level their deployment skills, not their combat stats.

Rallies

  • Dual Scarlett: her deployment skill Shadow Step cuts damage taken by allies, and it stays active in rallies. Treated as an S-tier rally leader.
  • Velocity Vixen: strong rally leader with an area patrol skill and a deployment skill that boosts demolition.
  • Techy Tony: his deployment skill lowers construction costs, so he’s worth leveling early.
  • Vault Trap uses a different hero list, so see the Events tab.

Arena

  • You can view an opponent’s defensive lineup from the Arena challenge history (added in a client update).
  • Revenge now follows a server list, not any loss from the current week.
  • Rank milestones pay 50 to 500 Diamonds from the top 500 down to rank 1.
  • Per-hero damage and healing stats were removed from result screens for some players.

Recent client changes

  • Auto-rally can be set to join only while you’re offline.
  • Alliance Mobilization: an Exclusive Tasks item lets you pick your task; task points were raised two to four times; an Elite alliance can be promoted to Glory with a top-3 finish and at least 7,000 points.
  • The “Recommended Ratio” rally setting and joining-rally troop-ratio option are limited or removed in many states.
  • Crime Boss rallies from Intel were removed; monster rallies need an alliance.
  • New R4 and R5 alliance chat tab; Hot Pursuit boss push notification.

Apply the vault split to every squad, but the first squad is the only one that may hit it exactly. Max Marksmen up to 70% in each deployment. The boss has 0 attack, so tanks add nothing there.

Counter triangle

  • Guard beats Biker, loses to Marksman.
  • Biker beats Marksman, loses to Guard.
  • Marksman beats Guard, loses to Biker.
  • Troop skills: all types get +10% damage vs their prey at T1. At T7+ Guards add +10% Defense vs Bikers, Bikers get a 20% chance to pierce Guards and hit Marksmen, and Marksmen get a 10% chance to strike twice.

For normal PvP, my suggestion (reasoned from the triangle, not from a guide): scout the enemy mix and tilt toward the class that beats their biggest share.

Troop economy

  • Deployment cap = march capacity x number of queues (max 6). Each deployment holds roughly 50k to 120k troops.
  • Over the cap: promote instead of training more. Under the cap: train fresh.
  • Promotion unlocks at division building level 13. Promote low tiers (T1 to T5) first; save T9 to T10 for training events.
  • T10 needs division level 30. Healing costs about 10% of training, so keep the Hospital from overflowing.

Rally joiners

  • Only the captain’s buffs apply to the whole rally. Joiners ride the leader’s research and gear.
  • Of the joiners, only the first skill of each player’s first hero counts, up to the 4 highest skill levels (ties go to who joined first). Exclusive equipment skills don’t apply when joining.
  • For Vault Trap only Andre and Velocity Vixen have a useful first skill (a Penetration boost). Level that skill and put one of them in slot one.

Etiquette that adds damage

Join the next open rally, don’t cherry-pick, and don’t start your own. Captains use the 5:00 gather timer (a full cycle is about 5:40) and follow the posted rotation. The last rally must start by about T-5:20.

Gear

  • Exclusive equipment: a 5th gear slot with 10 levels, unlocked at star 1. Gen 1 has it only for Brooke, Andre, Scarlett and Tony. It buffs only that hero’s own troop class and adds extra skill levels.
  • Gen 2: stars buff class Attack and Defense; exclusive gear adds class Health and Penetration plus two skills.
  • Chief Equipment: six pieces, and each only buffs one troop type. Build the set matching your main march. Upgrades cost Reinforced Metal and Tactical Gel.
  • Hero Gear: a 4-piece set per class, enhanced 0 to 100 and gated by Chief’s Office level.

Skills and upgrade order

  1. Hero level to your Police Department cap (80 at PD 25+).
  2. All key skills to level 3 before specializing.
  3. Exclusive equipment once the hero reaches star 1.
  4. Legendary gear and Forge (late game).

Push one hero to star 3 before spreading fragments. Don’t ignore Deployment skills, since a hero can be average in patrol and great on the state map.

Newer legendaries

Gen 3 (live from state day 112): Marcus (Guard) exclusive gives +15% garrison Defense, so he’s the defense anchor. Duke (Marksman) gives +15% rally Health. Lexie (Biker) gives +15% rally Attack. Keep Lexie out of the garrison.

Research hint

The Battle tree has 102 techs with per-class Attack, Defense, Health and Penetration. Examples: Defense Formation (Guard Defense) and Counterattack (Guard Attack).

Level 10 costs 75M each of Ammo and Cash. Costs jump hard after level 4.

Warehouse (max level 30)

Its capacity protects resources from being seized. Keep stockpiles under that limit before you go offline, or spend them on upgrades and training.

Hospital and Recovery Center

Wounded troops go to the Hospital. Past its capacity they overflow to the Recovery Center, which treats officers on the brink of death. Healing costs about 10% of training, so upgrade Hospital capacity before big events. The quick-select now remembers your last mix of troop types.

Dispatch Center

Sets how much alliance assistance and reinforcement you can send and receive. You can only send one reinforcement group to the same ally city.

Shields

  • PD protection shields from every source now show as one buff icon with the longest time left.
  • You can’t use a shield while your troops are supporting or stationed at the State Capitol, a Cannon, a Prison, or an alliance facility such as the Crime Lab or Arsenal. The reinforce buttons warn you first that the march cancels it.

Garrison heroes

  • Security Wall squad from one guide: Sunshine Lucy, Aegis Leon, Chef Gary (that page marks some sections as still pending).
  • Heroes can be marked “Stationed Only” on the Security Wall.
  • Garrison exclusive-gear skills: Marcus gives +15% garrison Defense, Samuel adds stationed-officer Attack, Pamela adds stationed-officer Health.
  • Guards get +10% Defense vs Bikers at T7 and above. Research Defense Formation (Guard Defense) and the Biker and Marksman defense techs.

Nothing I found gives a fixed troop split for the garrison. My own reasoning: a mixed home army avoids giving the enemy one class to exploit, but check this against your own battle reports.

Other habits

  • Keep troops home when a shield isn’t up. One guide suggests parking troops in an ally’s base if a server war is brewing. I couldn’t verify that, and it can cancel your shield at some facilities.
  • Turn on push notifications for Alliance Flag attacks.
  • Store reviews say new players get hit early, so build the Security Wall and Hospital before cosmetics.

Vault Trap quick setup

  • Troops: Marksman-heavy. One guide says 10% Guard, 20% Biker, 70% Marksman; the wiki says 1/9/90. The boss never hits back, so Guards add little.
  • Joiner slot: only your first (left) hero counts, and only its first skill. Use a hero whose first skill boosts damage: Velocity Vixen, Titan Andre, Hotshot Samuel or Furious Franklin; Pamela is decent (15% attack).
  • Stats that matter: Attack and Penetration. Defense is irrelevant here.
  • Upgrades: Vault Trap levels 1 to 5 add 5% attack each (max 25%) using donated Tranquilizers: 150, 200, 250, 300, 300 per level.
  • F2P rally heroes: Season 1: Aegis Leon, Dual Scarlett, Sunshine Lucy. Season 2: swap Leon for Hotshot Samuel.

Where F2P wins

  • Codes: redeem as soon as Chief’s Office 2 unlocks. One wiki showed no active codes on its last check, so look again when updates drop.
  • Daily free stuff: free recruits, patrols (Alloy comes from here), all Arena attempts. Save Arena Coins for Legendary Hero Equipment Chests.
  • Gather nonstop: keep at least one squad out, even offline. Ammo, Cash, Electricity and Gas gate almost everything.
  • Alliance perks: help, tech and gifts compound daily. Donate to alliance tech.

Where to spend a little

  • Second construction queue (best early value).
  • VIP 2: unlocks better VIP shop options per the early guides.
  • Avoid cosmetics; avoid random pulls with no goal hero.

Common mistakes

  • Upgrading random buildings instead of Chief’s Office prerequisites.
  • Letting academies go idle: troops are a huge share of power.
  • Spending speedups and Hardened Steel outside events that count them.
  • Splitting hero EXP across many heroes.
  • Leaving a full storehouse and idle troops exposed.

Late-game note

After server day 70, Electro Core is the big gate. Sources: daily tasks, intel cases, Hot Pursuit, Alliance Mobilization and Dawn of Justice, plus packs. Spend on Chief’s Office and academies first.
